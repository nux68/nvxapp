# Assistant voice commands
List of recognised commands, by application and topic

## Introduction
The **voice assistant** runs the most frequent operations without going through the menu: say (or type) what you want to do and the assistant recognises the command, asks for any missing information and runs the operation or opens the right page.

This list is built on the commands **available to you**: it only contains those for your roles and for the applications active for your company. There is one chapter per application, split by topic; at the end you will find the alphabetical index of the commands.

### How to use it
1. Press the assistant button at the bottom right and choose the **microphone** (or the text chat).
2. Hold the button down to speak, or switch the microphone on and speak freely.
3. Say what you want to do in your own words: the sample phrases are only a guide.
4. If some information is missing the assistant asks for it; for operations that change data it shows a summary and asks for confirmation.

> Can't remember a command? Say **help** or **what can you do**: the assistant lists the commands, starting with those useful on the page you are on.

### What you can ask
Each active application adds its own commands, which come in four kinds:
- **Questions**: deadlines, status, summaries and information about a person or an item ("which deadlines are due in the next 30 days?"). They answer straight away and change nothing.
- **Entries**: simple operations that add or update data. The assistant shows a summary of what it is about to do and goes ahead only after your confirmation, which you can also give by voice ("yes", "confirm"); if you answer "no" nothing is saved.
- **Opening a record**: "open ..." followed by the kind of item and its name or code opens the record directly, to view or edit it.
- **Navigation**: "take me to ...", "go to ..." or "open the ... page" opens the page you name, including the manual of an application.

Users who only access their personal area find the commands about themselves, usually introduced by "my".

### What the assistant sees
- The assistant reads data with the same rules as the pages: it only answers about what you could see by opening the matching page, with your roles.
- Some confidential data is not available by voice and can only be viewed from the page: in that case the assistant tells you and offers to open it.
- Long lists are shortened: the assistant says so, and for the full list it is better to open the page.

### Tips
- You can give several details in the same sentence (for example the person, the day and the time): the assistant recognises them all together.
- Dates and times can be said naturally: "today", "yesterday", "last Monday", "at half past eight", "by 30 June", "in the next 60 days".
- People, customers and items are named by their name (the surname alone is enough): if more than one matches, the assistant asks which one you mean.
- Some words apply to several applications (for example deadlines, settings, documents, employees): if the assistant cannot tell which one you mean, add the name of the application or of the topic.
- After a period of inactivity the microphone switches off by itself: switch it back on with the button.
- Spoken replies are switched on and off from the speaker icon in the assistant window.
